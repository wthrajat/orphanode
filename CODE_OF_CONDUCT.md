# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Orphanode project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience; and
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
  and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces. Representation includes using an
official project account, posting through an official channel, or acting as an
appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainers
using a private contact method published on the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/wthrajat). If none is
available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel without
including sensitive incident details. GitHub's report-abuse and blocking tools
are also available for conduct on GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly as maintainer
capacity allows. Maintainers must respect the privacy and security of reporters.
Anyone enforcing this policy must recuse themselves from a report in which they
have a conflict of interest.

Consequences will be proportionate to the nature, impact, and persistence of the
behavior. They may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction,
or permanent ban from project interaction.
